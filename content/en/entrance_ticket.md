Free entrance to our volunteer organizers or associates of [Associação Inércia](https://inercia.pt) or [SFIA](https://incrivelalmadense.pt) with their yearly quotas paid.

10€ entrance fee to other attendants (5€ each day).

Sunday morning is free entrance.

Want to really support the event? We have a special supporters ticket costing 100€, it includes several Inércia merchandise and that warm fuzzy feeling that you're supporting the organization of this and future events organized by Associação Inércia.
